# Trademark policy (DRAFT)

> Draft for discussion. To be reviewed by counsel and adapted once the mark is filed (OAPI, ARIPO, EUIPO). The name **Ubao** is the selected candidate, not yet validated (native speaker, trademark search).

The code is licensed under its open-source licence. **The name "Ubao", the logo, and the "Certified" badges are not covered by that licence.** They are protected as trademarks of the project owner (entity to be defined, ADR-0009).

## 1. What you can do without asking
- Say that your product is *compatible with* or *built on* Ubao, in plain text.
- Name the software accurately in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons.
- Distribute unmodified official builds with the name intact.
- Use the name for community material (meetups, translations, tutorials) if it does not suggest official endorsement.

## 2. Forks and modified builds
- You may fork and redistribute the code under the licence.
- A modified or forked distribution **must not** be called "Ubao" nor use the logo or a confusingly similar name or logo. Remove the branding assets and pick your own name; say "based on Ubao" if accurate.
- Patched builds for internal use inside one organisation may keep the name if the changes are limited to configuration, translation and packaging.

## 3. Hosting providers
- You may host Ubao for customers (subject to the licensing model, see LICENSING.md) but must describe the service as "hosted Ubao" or "Ubao-based service from <your brand>", not as the official Ubao service.
- Using the name in your own product or domain name (for example `ubao-<yourbrand>`) requires written permission.
- Hosters that pass the partner programme may display "Ubao Certified Partner" under the partner agreement.

## 4. Certified partners and plugins
- "Certified Partner" (deploy, admin, facilitator tracks) and plugin tiers **Core / Verified / Community / Private** are marks controlled by the project. Use is granted by agreement and may be revoked, for example on security or conduct violations.
- Plugin authors may state "for Ubao" and use the plugin tier badge only if the registry shows that tier for their package.
- Plugins must not imply they are first-party.

## 5. Not allowed
- Registering domains, social handles, app-store listings, or company names containing Ubao.
- Using the logo as your own branding, or in a way that suggests endorsement.
- Selling "official" support, certificates or training without a partner agreement.

## 6. Logo
Logo files are distributed in a brand kit with their own terms (not MIT). Do not alter proportions, colours or add effects.

## 7. Contact and enforcement
Questions and permission requests: to be defined (legal contact address). We prefer to resolve misuse by a friendly notice first.
