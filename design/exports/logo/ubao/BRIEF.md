# Logo brief — Ubao

## Name
- **Ubao** — Swahili, *board / plank*; *ubao* is also the everyday word for the classroom blackboard. Pronounced *u-BA-o* (3 syllables).
- Written lowercase in the wordmark: **ubao**. Backup name: Kikao ("session / meeting").
- To validate: Swahili native speakers (connotations), trademark search OAPI / ARIPO / EUIPO / INPI / USPTO.

## Product (one sentence)
Open-source (MIT) collaborative workspace — Notion-like docs + Miro-like infinite canvas — local-first, works offline and on slow networks, self-hostable / sovereign, built for African teams, startups and public administrations.

## Audience
Startups & tech SMEs, public administrations (ministries, agencies), facilitators; francophone + anglophone Africa; low-end laptops and Android phones.

## Brand adjectives
calm · precise · premium (luxury = restraint) · quietly African · trustworthy / sovereign · collaborative.

## Visual system (fixed)
- Colour: **Malachite** #0B5D49 (dark theme #3FBF95) as the only accent; **gold** #B08A2E as rare ornament (Sovereign edition); ink #0F1211; paper #FAFAF7.
- Type: Inter (UI), Fraunces (display, once per screen), JetBrains Mono. Logo letters must be **constructed as paths**, not live text.
- Iconography: Lucide-like 1.5 px line, rounded joins → logo must sit naturally next to that.
- Motif "Fil": abstracted strip-weaving rhythm and bogolan marks (never literal).

## Story hooks (from the name)
- *Ubao* = the **board** where people gather to write and decide (blackboard, the board of a meeting, the plank).
- The **bao** board game (East-African mancala, "bao la kiswahili") is played on a carved wooden board with rows of pits — a shared surface where two minds meet. Use only as an *abstract* rhythm (rows of points), never a literal game board.
- Plank / wood grain: a horizontal plane, a surface to build on.

## Must work
16 px favicon & tray icon, app icon (rounded square), one colour, reversed (on malachite), dark mode, gold Sovereign variant, horizontal lockup symbol + wordmark, stacked lockup. Next to Lucide icons in a 264 px sidebar at 24 px.

## Avoid (category & cultural clichés)
Sticky-note icon, speech bubbles, pencil, light bulb, puzzle, people-in-a-circle, map of Africa, sun/savanna, masks, literal kente/adinkra copies, flag greens, cream + serif + terracotta template, purple AI gradients, Instagram-like concentric rounded squares, anything resembling an "edit" icon or a checkbox.

## Previous attempt (rejected)
"wiro" open-frame + dot (archived in `_archive-wiro/`) — rejected: wrong name, and too close to a generic "C + dot" glyph.
