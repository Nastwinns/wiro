5. "Conçu pour l'Afrique": hors ligne, faible débit (data meter), FR/EN + langues locales, prix en monnaie locale.
5b. **Carte du monde « Là où nous commençons »** (designed, Penpot section `reach-map`): dot-matrix world map (Fil motif, neutral grey, 3 px squares), **heat zones** as stepped concentric rings in gold ornament (no gradients, malachite stays for actions), numbered pins with leader lines, legend in two rows (launch / second wave / listening; then extension by region). Targeted cities: Dakar (launch), Abidjan, Nairobi, Accra, Lagos; extension: Addis-Abeba, Johannesburg, Casablanca, Paris, Londres, Bruxelles, São Paulo, Bangkok, Shanghai. Map is decorative + data: alt text lists the cities; legend is the accessible equivalent; on 390 px the legend stacks and the map crops to a horizontal scroll or a simplified view (to design).
6. Souveraineté: déploiement sur vos serveurs / cloud national, air-gapped, audit, code ouvert MIT ou Apache-2.0 → CTA "Parler à un partenaire".
7. Performance proof: "10 000 objets à 60 i/s sur un portable d'entrée de gamme" (benchmark link).
8. Open source block: GitHub stars, contributors map, licence MIT ou Apache-2.0 (au choix).
9. Testimonials (only real), FAQ, final CTA, footer (partners, docs, status, security, legal, language switch).

## S-14.2 Pricing
**Local price rule (decided 2026-11-11):** prices are EUR-parity (XOF is pegged: 655,957 XOF/EUR), converted at the central-bank reference of 11 Nov 2026 — CBN 1 514,29 NGN/EUR, CBK 145,68 KES/EUR — then rounded; reviewed quarterly (NGN is volatile). Resulting grid: Team 2 000 XOF · 4 600 NGN · 450 KES · 3 € per editor/month; Business ≈ 4 000 XOF · 9 200 NGN · 900 KES · 6 €; pack 10 editors 20 000 XOF · 46 000 NGN · 4 500 KES · 30 €; Sovereign from 12 000 €/yr (≈ 18 M NGN, ≈ 1,75 M KES) up to 250 users. Tiers from market study (Community, Personal Cloud, Team, Business, Sovereign, Services); currency switch (XOF / NGN / KES / EUR / USD) with round local prices; "Qui est facturé ?" block; FAQ (guests free, no auto-upgrade, quarterly billing).
